## Context

What situation or problem made this decision necessary? What
constraints (compliance regime, blast radius, team size, existing
tooling, timeline) shaped the options that were even on the table? Be
specific enough that someone with zero prior context on this project
could read this section alone and understand why a decision was needed
at all.

## Decision

What did you decide, stated as a clear, single sentence if possible?

## Options Considered

- **Option A** -- description, and why it was or wasn't chosen
- **Option B** -- description, and why it was or wasn't chosen
- **Option C** -- description, and why it was or wasn't chosen

A real ADR names genuine alternatives that were seriously considered,
not strawmen included only to make the chosen option look better. If
you can't articulate a real reason someone reasonable would have
proposed an option, it probably doesn't belong here.

## Consequences

What does this decision cost (complexity, reduced capability, an
operational burden someone now owns)? What does it enable? What would
have to change in the future for this decision to need revisiting --
and who would need to be involved in that revisit, given this org's
compliance constraints?

## Compliance Note (if applicable)

Does this decision touch a regulated system, a control a compliance
framework names explicitly, or an audit requirement? If so, name the
specific requirement (not just "compliance requires this") and how
this decision satisfies it. If this decision has NO compliance
dimension, say so explicitly rather than leaving the section out --
an ADR that's silent on compliance is easy to mistake for one that
considered it and found nothing.
